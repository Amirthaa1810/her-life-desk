# 🌸 Her Life Desk 2.0

### A Digital Companion for Women to Know • Own • Protect • Earn • Grow

**Her Life Desk 2.0** is a personal digital companion designed to bring essential resources, awareness, opportunities, safety tools, learning, financial guidance, career support, and community features into one unified platform.

Instead of women having to search across multiple platforms for different kinds of support, Her Life Desk brings these resources together in a single, accessible and user-friendly digital space.

🔗 **Live Website:** https://her-life-desk.vercel.app/

---

## 🌟 Why Her Life Desk?

Women often need information and support across many different areas of life — from education and careers to financial awareness, legal rights, digital safety and personal growth.

However, these resources are often scattered across different websites and platforms.

**Her Life Desk 2.0 addresses this gap by providing a centralized digital companion built around five core pillars:**

> **Know • Own • Protect • Earn • Grow**

### 🧠 Know

Access educational resources, awareness content, news and learning opportunities.

### 🏠 Own

Build awareness around personal identity, ownership, independence and important life decisions.

### 🛡️ Protect

Learn about digital safety, legal awareness and personal protection.

### 💰 Earn

Explore career opportunities, jobs, financial resources and entrepreneurship.

### 🌱 Grow

Track personal development, learning, goals and long-term growth.

# 🧩 Technology Stack

| Technology        | Purpose                                            |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Next.js**       | Full-stack React framework and application routing |
| **React**         | Building reusable user interface components        |
| **TypeScript**    | Type-safe application development                  |
| **Tailwind CSS**  | Responsive and utility-based styling               |
| **React Icons**   | Interface icons                                    |
| **Node.js / npm** | Development environment and package management     |
| **Vercel**        | Deployment and hosting                             |

The repository is structured as a Next.js project using the App Router and is configured with TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.
